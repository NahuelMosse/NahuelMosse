# Accessibility statement

Saved as `docs/business/accessibility.md` and published with the product when it is public (criterion I17).
Written in the language of the users, kept current: a card that changes what the statement says changes the
statement. The conformance report uses the four terms of VPAT 2.5Rev INT: Supports, Partially Supports, Does
Not Support, Not Applicable. Rules: `reference/accessibility.md` (A11Y-PRO-*). The legal context (Ley 26.653
in Argentina, the European Accessibility Act, EN 301 549) informs the text; it is not a method obligation.
