# Legal: what a card ships must be allowed to ship

Rules a card can meet and a check can verify, for a team in Argentina with clients in AR, LatAm, the US and the EU.
**Not legal advice.** A line marked **[lawyer]** is decided by a person with counsel, never by the agent: it is an `AMB` on
the card, or a line for a person in `closing.md`. Each rule names its source; a source marked *(secondary)* was read only in
secondary sources and is confirmed against the official text before it becomes a hard check.

## 1. Code from others

Licences of dependencies and of copied code are `dependencies.md` §3 (LIC): allowed, review and blocked lists, the
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` generated, never written by hand.

- **LEG-01** — Code copied from another repo or answer carries, beside the copy, its licence text (a `LICENSE` in the ported
  folder) or an `SPDX-License-Identifier` with the original copyright and the source URL; a fragment inside an own file goes
  between `SPDX-SnippetBegin` / `SPDX-SnippetEnd` (REUSE 3.3). Code with no licence is all rights reserved: it is not copied.
  Stack Overflow code is CC BY-SA: credited, and kept out of a file with another licence. · Check: `reuse lint` where the repo
  uses REUSE; otherwise a grep of `Source:`/`SPDX` in the files the card copied.
- **LEG-02** — A port from a repo reads its `LICENSE` first, and its trademark or asset notes (`DISCLAIMER.md`, per-file
  licences). An icon set under CC0 covers the drawing, not the brand.
- **LEG-03** — Code written by the agent is marked in the commit (`Co-Authored-By`). Who owns code generated by AI, and
  whether it is protected, is **[lawyer]** per client contract.

## 2. Assets

Sources and credits are `assets.md` (`CREDITS.md`, one line per file: source URL, author, licence, credit required).

- **LEG-04** — Every image, video, font and icon the build ships has its line; `CC-BY-NC-*` and `-ND` are not shipped in a
  commercial product. A generated asset names its generator and model, and shows no real face, no brand and no artwork
  of a living artist that the card does not own. · Check: every file under `public/`, `assets/` and `fonts/` appears in
  `CREDITS.md`.
- **LEG-05** — A font is self-hosted under a licence that allows it (OFL-1.1, or the foundry's licence read for that family;
  Fontshare and other foundries license per family). Loading fonts from `fonts.googleapis.com`/`fonts.gstatic.com` sends
  the visitor's IP to a third party without consent, which a German court found contrary to the GDPR (LG München I,
  3 O 17493/20, 2022-01-20).
  · Check: no `fonts.googleapis|fonts.gstatic|use.typekit` in the shipped HTML and CSS.
- **LEG-06** — A third-party logo appears only for what the product really has (a payment, a login, an integration), under
  that brand's guidelines; never as a client, partner or press it does not have.

`node .claude/tools/legal-check.mjs [root]` reports LEG-04, LEG-05 and the trackers of LEG-08.

## 3. Privacy and cookies

The data inventory, the subject's rights and breach notification are `security.md` (SEC-DAT, SEC-IR); the applicable norm is
named in `system-design.md`.

- **LEG-07** — A site that collects personal data or sets non-essential cookies links a privacy page from every footer:
  who is responsible, what is collected and why, the legal basis, recipients and transfers, retention, rights and the
  contact. In Argentina, Ley 25.326 and the AAIP; in the EU, GDPR arts. 13–14 (it applies to an Argentine company that
  offers services to people in the EU); in Brazil, the LGPD. · Check: the privacy URL answers 200 and the footer links it.
- **LEG-08** — No analytics, ad or tracking cookie, and no request to a tracker, before consent (ePrivacy art. 5(3);
  EDPB guidelines 2/2023, version 2.0 of 2024-10-07). Rejecting is as easy as accepting. · Check: Playwright with a clean context, without accepting:
  no `_ga`, `_gid`, `_fbp`, `_gcl_*` cookie and no request to `google-analytics.com`, `googletagmanager.com`,
  `connect.facebook.net`, `doubleclick.net`, `hotjar.com` or `clarity.ms`.
- **LEG-09** — A form that collects personal data says what for, next to the submit; a consent box for anything beyond the
  request (newsletter, sharing) starts unchecked.
- **LEG-10** — Registering databases with the AAIP, international transfers, the EU representative and the DPA with each
  provider are **[lawyer]**.

## 4. Consumers

- **LEG-11** — A site that sells online to consumers in Argentina shows a "Botón de arrepentimiento" link and, for
  subscriptions, a "Botón de baja" link, visible on first access, with no prior registration or any other additional step
  (Disposición 954/2025, BO 2025-09-04, which repealed Res. 424/2020 and 316/2018 of the former Secretaría de Comercio
  Interior; Ley 24.240 art. 34: 10 calendar days to revoke). In the EU, a withdrawal
  function in the online interface from 2026-06-19 (Directive 2023/2673, art. 11a of 2011/83), 14 days of withdrawal, and
  the total price with taxes and shipping. · Check: Playwright finds the links on the home at the first viewport.
- **LEG-12** — Terms, refunds and clauses with consumers of another country are **[lawyer]**.

## 5. Email

- **LEG-13** — A commercial email carries a working unsubscribe link and a `List-Unsubscribe` header (one-click,
  RFC 8058), the sender's postal address (CAN-SPAM), and an honest subject; an unsubscribe is honoured within 10 business
  days. In Argentina, Ley 26.951 (Registro No Llame) covers offers by telephone, not email *(secondary)*. · Check: a grep of each template for the
  unsubscribe link and the address.

## 6. Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA is the target (`accessibility.md`). It is a legal duty under the European Accessibility Act for products and
e-commerce sold in the EU from 2025-06-28, under the ADA in the US, and under Ley 26.653 for the Argentine state and its
contractors. The automatic checks (axe) cover part of WCAG; the rest is reviewed by a person.

## 7. Security with legal weight

Secrets never in the repo (`gate-checks.mjs` reports credentials in the source; `gitleaks` in CI where the repo has it), and
known vulnerabilities audited (`npm audit`, `osv-scanner`). The EU Cyber Resilience Act asks a product with digital elements
sold in the EU to report actively exploited vulnerabilities from 2026-09-11 and to meet its full duties from 2027-12-11 *(secondary)*;
whether a client's product falls under it is **[lawyer]**.

## 8. Contracts

Assignment or licence of the delivered code's IP, the inventory of open-source parts handed to the client, and liability
are **[lawyer]**: the kit delivers the inventory (`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, `CREDITS.md`), not the clause.
